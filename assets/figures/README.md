# Figure provenance

- `trip_times.png`, `coverage_four_methods.png`, `toy_rmse.png`, and
  `beam_sampling_variability.png` are generated by
  `notebooks/StrategicSampling.ipynb` with fixed random seeds.
- `research_all_n_discrepancy.png` is copied without modification from
  `MCQMC26/notebooks/artifacts/figures/special_d64_kuo_new_lattice_new_kronecker_discrepancy.png`
  in Fred J. Hickernell's public MCQMC 2026 repository. It appears in the
  MCQMC 2026 plenary deck's section “Sequences that are good for all n”.
  The figure compares a Kuo lattice, a new lattice, and a new Kronecker
  construction for the plotted weighted discrepancy criterion in d=64.
- The original `iid_uniform_pts.png` and `lattice_pts.png` came from the
  QMCSoftware “Why Add Q to MC?” article and are retained for reference.
